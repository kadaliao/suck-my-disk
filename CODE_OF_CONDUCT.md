# Code of conduct · 行为准则

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

SuckMyDisk follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
([translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/)).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the
work and not the person, and remember that people write here in their second
language. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at kadaliao@gmail.com. Reports are
handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and warn,
suspend or ban participants, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.

## 简体中文

SuckMyDisk 采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)（[其他语言版本](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/)）作为行为准则。

简单说：友善、有耐心，先假定对方是好意；批评针对事，不针对人；很多人在用第二语言交流，多体谅一点。在 issue、PR、讨论区以及其他代表本项目的地方，不欢迎骚扰、辱骂和人身攻击。

遇到问题请发邮件给维护者：kadaliao@gmail.com。所有举报都会私下处理。维护者可以按 Contributor Covenant 的执行指引编辑或删除评论，对参与者给予警告、暂时禁止或永久禁止参与。
